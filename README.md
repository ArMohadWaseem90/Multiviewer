## 🧭 What Multiviewer Does

Multiviewer takes multiple video sources and places them into one clean view. You can use it to monitor several cameras at once, watch a screen capture alongside a live feed, or check different video inputs without opening a separate player for each one. The layout is designed to be simple: add your sources, choose how you want them arranged, and start watching.

The application supports several video protocols and device types. That means it can work with different kinds of video equipment and software outputs, not just one brand or one format. Whether your video comes from a network stream, a capture card, or a screen capture, Multiviewer is built to bring those feeds together in one place.
